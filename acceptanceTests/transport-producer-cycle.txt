; Transport producer cycle
;
; A city that launched a transport keeps building armies until that specific
; transport enters sail-to-unload, then returns to transport production.

;===============================================================
; Transport producer city builds armies while its latest transport is loading.
;===============================================================
GIVEN game map
  X~taaaaaa
GIVEN X belongs to country 1.
GIVEN t belongs to country 1.
GIVEN a1 belongs to country 1.
GIVEN a2 belongs to country 1.
GIVEN a3 belongs to country 1.
GIVEN a4 belongs to country 1.
GIVEN a5 belongs to country 1.
GIVEN a6 belongs to country 1.
GIVEN t has produced-at [0 0].
GIVEN t has transport-mission loading.

WHEN the computer chooses production at X.

THEN production at X is army.

GIVEN a2 belongs to country 1.

GIVEN a4 belongs to country 1.
GIVEN a5 belongs to country 1.
GIVEN a6 belongs to country 1.
GIVEN t has produced-at [0 0].
GIVEN t has transport-mission sail-to-unload.

WHEN the computer chooses production at X.

THEN production at X is transport.
