; Transport unloading hold
;
; After first landing, a transport should hold position and keep trying to
; unload in place for four rounds before resuming crawl.

;===============================================================
; Unloading transport holds position before crawling away.
;===============================================================
GIVEN game map
  ####
  t~~~
GIVEN computer map
  ####
  t~~~
GIVEN t has transport-mission unloading.
GIVEN t has 2 armies.
GIVEN t has unloading-hold-since-round 10.
GIVEN round-number is 13.

WHEN computer transport t is processed.

THEN t is at [1 1].
THEN t has transport-mission sail-to-load.

;===============================================================
; Unloading transport breaks hold after four rounds and resumes crawling.
;===============================================================
GIVEN game map
  ####
  t~~~
GIVEN computer map
  ####
  t~~~
GIVEN t has transport-mission unloading.
GIVEN t has 2 armies.
GIVEN t has unloading-hold-since-round 10.
GIVEN round-number is 14.

WHEN computer transport t is processed.

THEN t is at [1 1].
THEN t has transport-mission sail-to-load.
