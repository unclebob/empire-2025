; Transport leave-city
;
; Newly produced computer transports leave their city before entering sail-to-load.

;===============================================================
; Leave-city transport moves to coastal sea and enters sail-to-load.
;===============================================================
GIVEN game map
  Xt
  ~~
GIVEN computer map
  Xt
  ~~
GIVEN t has transport-mission leave-city.

WHEN computer transport t is processed.

THEN t is at [1 0].
THEN t has transport-mission sail-to-load.
