; Transport loading manifest
;
; Returning transports wait at their chosen loading coast for recruited armies.

;===============================================================
; Sail-to-load transport enters loading when it reaches its target coast.
;===============================================================
GIVEN game map
  a#t~
  ###X
GIVEN computer map
  a#t~
  ###X
GIVEN t has transport-mission sail-to-load.
GIVEN t has load-target-cell [3 1].
GIVEN t has sail-path [].

WHEN a new round starts.

THEN t is at [2 0].
THEN t has transport-mission loading.
