; Satellite lifecycle tests.
; Covers turns-remaining decrement and satellite destruction.

;===============================================================
; Satellite with 2 turns remaining has 1 after a round.
;===============================================================
GIVEN game map
  V~~~~~~~~~~~=
GIVEN V has turns-remaining 2.
GIVEN V's target is =.
GIVEN player units V.

WHEN a new round starts.

THEN V has 1 turns remaining.

;===============================================================
; Satellite with 1 turn remaining is destroyed after a round.
;===============================================================
GIVEN game map
  V~~~~~~~~~~~=
GIVEN V has turns-remaining 1.
GIVEN V's target is =.
GIVEN player units V.

WHEN a new round starts.

THEN there is no V on the map.

;===============================================================
; Satellite moves toward target and decrements turns-remaining.
;===============================================================
GIVEN game map
  V~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~=
GIVEN V has turns-remaining 10.
GIVEN V's target is =.
GIVEN player units V.

WHEN a new round starts.

THEN V is at [10 0].
THEN V has 9 turns remaining.

;===============================================================
; Computer satellite with direction moves and decrements.
;===============================================================
GIVEN game map
  v~~~~~~~~~~~#
GIVEN v has direction [1 0].
GIVEN v has turns-remaining 5.

WHEN a new round starts.

THEN v is at [10 0].
THEN v has 4 turns remaining.
